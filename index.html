About the Run

Run through the vibrant streets of Kerala's capital in a marathon that celebrates fitness, unity, and community spirit.

On 11 October 2026, the BSNL 5K Mini Marathon invites runners of every age and fitness level to experience Thiruvananthapuram like never before — a scenic 5K course that winds through the heart of the city, starting and finishing at the BSNL Kerala Circle Office. Organized by BSNL Trivandrum BA, this event is a tribute to fitness, unity, and the spirit of community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate? +
Everyone! The 5K is open to all age groups and fitness levels — whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned runner.
What are the age categories? +
There are four competitive categories: Men 15–45 years, Women 15–45 years, Men above 45 years, and Women above 45 years. Age is taken as on race day, 11 October 2026, so carry a photo ID for verification at bib collection.
Is this a timed race? +
No. This event uses a non-timed BIB for participation identification only — there is no individual chip timing or GPS route tracking.
What does the registration fee include? +
Your fee covers the event T-shirt, bib number, finisher medal, hydration on route, and refreshments at the finish line.
What is the early bird pricing? +
Registrations open 21 September 2026. Register within the first 10 days (by 1 October 2026) to pay ₹600. From 2 October 2026 onward, the fee is ₹700.
How do I get my bib number and T-shirt? +
Kit collection details will be shared with all registered participants closer to the event date via WhatsApp/email.
Is there a last date for registration? +
Registrations close once we hit capacity or a few days before the event — so it's best to register early!
